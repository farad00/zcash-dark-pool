My Predictions

This vault keeps independent credentials on this browser and device. It creates no server account.

Keep control of your credential

The ticket secret controls claim/refund capability. Anyone who obtains a finalized ticket credential may control its value.

Individual ticket backup and restore are available for completed issuance with a saved local binding. Drafts and pending attempts cannot be exported. Losing or clearing browser storage or device data can cause permanent loss of locally held claim capability. The server cannot recover your claim secret.

Browser storage is not encrypted by this app or hardware-backed. Browser extensions, malware or a compromised client can read it.

Local records

A draft holds a locally generated secret. It has no server-issued ticket ID and is not an accepted prediction. A finalized local credential has final IDs supplied by a later issuance flow; it does not prove funding or claim eligibility.

Ticket issuance, prediction submission, payments and claims are unavailable. Creating a local draft only saves a fresh secret on this device.
