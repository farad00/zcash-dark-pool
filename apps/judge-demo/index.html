Frozen rules & resolution

YES: the synthetic observation is recorded YES. NO: it is recorded NO. An ambiguous or unavailable observation means VOID and refunds. This is a controlled rehearsal, not a real-world forecast.

Each new rehearsal fixes a 30-second issuance window before opening. Timely simulated exact payments require three simulated confirmations before that window closes. Late or incomplete simulations are excluded. This browser policy is separate from the local server’s chain-boundary implementation.

Accepted stakes: 0.00000001–1 simulated ZEC. Up to 20 tickets. No house fee. Network fees are externally subsidized in the calculation; this demo creates no transaction. If the winning side has no stake, accepted stakes are refunded.

THE DESIGN / THE BOUNDARIES

Privacy with clear limits.

Why Zcash?

A shielded payment system can keep the blockchain from becoming a public prediction book. The intended product allocates a fresh shielded receiver per ticket and matches notes with a view-only watcher.

Positions, stake sizes and payouts are intended to be hidden from the public blockchain and other market participants. The market operator has the viewing/application access required for settlement.

This simulation does not send Zcash and does not prove those blockchain properties.

What you can verify here

The existing browser vault, original claim-commitment format, individual ticket backup/import, blinded ledger commitment and exact integer-zatoshi settlement calculator.

Deposit detection, confirmations, oracle evidence, claim reservation and payout intents are browser simulations. They do not establish server concurrency guarantees, external publication, solvency, genuine deposits or payouts.

What remains trusted

The operator can see the private book and supplies the manual oracle. Browser/IP/timing metadata is not hidden. Browser storage is unencrypted and same-origin scripts or extensions may read it.

No operator-blind, trustless, audited-production or active FROST claim. No identity-based secret recovery.
